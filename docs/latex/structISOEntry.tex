\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Kernel-\/internal representation of a parsed Interrupt Source Override. 

Populated during MADT parsing and used by \doxylink{apic_8cpp_adc9bb3dd155adce97361c2fbac6e2732}{Init\+IOAPIC()} to correctly route legacy ISA IRQs to their actual GSI lines on the I/\+O APIC. Up to 16 ISOs are stored, matching the maximum number of ISA IRQ lines.
